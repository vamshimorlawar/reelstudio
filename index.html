Your own faceless video studio, on your Mac.

Reel Studio writes, voices, edits and schedules short videos (Reels, Shorts, carousels and stories) from a few series you define. It runs on your computer, with your keys, using free and open-source tools.

Everything a faceless channel needs

From the idea to the uploaded Short, each step is automated, and you can change any of it.

🗂️

Series you design

Define each series once: topic, audience, tone, format, hooks, footage and hashtags. A weekly grid decides what gets made each day.

✍️

Scripts with guardrails

Claude writes first, Gemini is the free fallback. Every script is checked: no financial or medical advice, English only, timed to the length you want.

🎙️

Natural voice and captions

A free, high-quality local voice (Kokoro) with sentence or word-by-word captions in your fonts and colours.

🪝

Hooks and endings that work

Big hook text in the first seconds, closing lines that rotate between share, comment, save and follow, and a ready-to-pin first comment.

🐶

Talking mascot

Five original cartoon characters that lip-sync to the voiceover, blink and bob in a corner of the video.

🧩

Puzzle format

The puzzle on a clean card with a spoken 5-4-3-2-1 countdown. The answer is never in the video; it's revealed with the next puzzle.

🖼️

Carousels and stories

Swipeable 4:5 carousels and 9:16 story sets in the same style, with space left for poll and link stickers.

📈

Trend Scout

Google Trends, Wikipedia and YouTube data turned into fresh, on-brand topics for each series.

▶️

YouTube auto-posting

Uploads Shorts on your schedule through YouTube's official API, after a review window so you can reject anything first.

📁

Your files, your Mac

Everything lands in Finder folders: To post, Posted and Rejected. Your own clips and music go in the Media Library.

Built to be safe

Responsible content

  • Scripts can't give financial, investment or medical advice; off-limits topics are blocked before anything is written.
  • No invented statistics or quotes; claims are hedged and sources are listed for checking.
  • Every caption discloses the AI voice, and YouTube's AI-content label is set automatically on upload.
  • Stock footage and photos come from Pixabay under its free commercial licence, or from your own Media Library.

Private by design

  • Runs locally on your Mac. Videos, keys and sign-ins stay on it; only the text needed is sent to the AI writer and stock search.
  • Posts to YouTube through the official API, with a review window first.
  • No accounts, no cloud, no tracking. This website has no cookies or analytics either.
